Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026 — Studio Converter Pro Suite v5.2.8

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what data is processed when you use Studio Converter Pro Suite (available at https://studioconverterpro.pages.dev, both as a website and as an Android app) and how it is used.

1. Data controller

Data controller: PNX
Contact email: supportpnx007@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

The app runs almost entirely locally, on your device. In particular:

  • File conversions: images, videos and audio are processed in your browser / on your device. The files you upload are not stored on our servers.
  • Audio extraction from video: this runs entirely in your browser: the video is never sent to a server.
  • Account: if you create an account, we store only your username and the SHA-256 hash of your password (never the plain password) in an encrypted database (Cloudflare KV), so the same account works on PC, phone and app. On your device the password is still stored as a hash in the browser.
  • Push notifications: if you enable notifications, the device is registered by topic (e.g. "updates") through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging. We collect or store no email addresses, phone numbers or personal identities for notifications: they only tell you when a new version is out or when something changed on the site.
  • Version and updates: on startup the app downloads a small file (version.json) with the current version, to offer you updates. It contains no personal data.

3. Cookies and tracking

We do not use profiling cookies, advertising or analytics tools. We only use:

  • localStorage for login, preferences and language;
  • service worker cache to make the app work offline;
  • IndexedDB/cache to keep the files you choose to archive on your device.

4. Third-party services

  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages / Workers / KV) — hosting of the site, of the server functions (login, notifications) and of the account database (IP address and technical access logs handled by Cloudflare).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivery of update push notifications (handled by Google, under its own privacy policy).
  • CDNs (cdnjs / jsDelivr) — provide the JavaScript libraries required by the app.
  • GitHub — public project repository and APK update releases.
  • MediaFire — hosts the downloadable desktop app package.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

5. Data retention

We do not store uploaded files or converted content. Accounts stay in the database until you ask for their deletion. Push notifications are sent only when the site/version changes and you can turn them off at any time from your device.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You can at any time:

  • ask for information about the data concerning you;
  • request the deletion of your account and of any data;
  • withdraw your consent to processing.

Simply write to supportpnx007@gmail.com. For data stored in your browser you can delete it yourself by clearing site data/cache or uninstalling the PWA. You can also disable notifications from your system settings.

7. Changes

This privacy policy is updated whenever something relevant changes on the site or the app (for example when the version changes). The current version is always published on this page.

8. Contact

Questions, requests or reports: supportpnx007@gmail.com
