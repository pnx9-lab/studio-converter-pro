Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026 — Studio Converter Pro Suite v5.2.3

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what data is processed when you use Studio Converter Pro Suite (available at https://studioconverterpro.netlify.app) and how it is used.

1. Data controller

Data controller: PNX
Contact email: supportpnx007@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

The app runs almost entirely locally, on your device. In particular:

  • File conversions: images, videos and audio are processed in your browser / on your device. The files you upload are not stored on our servers.
  • Local account: if you create an account, your username and password stay in your browser's localStorage (the password is stored as a hash, never in plain text). No account data is sent to us.
  • Audio extraction from video: when you use the server-side feature, the file is sent temporarily for processing, returned to you and deleted immediately afterwards.

3. Cookies and tracking

We do not use profiling cookies, advertising or analytics tools. We only use:

  • localStorage for login and preferences;
  • service worker cache to make the app work offline.

4. Third-party services

  • Netlify — hosting of the site and of the processing functions (IP address and technical access logs handled by Netlify).
  • CDNs (cdnjs / jsDelivr) — provide the JavaScript libraries required by the app.
  • GitHub — public project repository and download releases.
  • MediaFire — hosts the downloadable desktop app package.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

5. Data retention

We do not store uploaded files or converted content. Files sent for audio extraction are automatically deleted once processing is complete.

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You can at any time:

  • ask for information about the data concerning you;
  • request the deletion of any data;
  • withdraw your consent to processing.

Simply write to supportpnx007@gmail.com. For data stored in your browser you can delete it yourself by clearing site data/cache or uninstalling the PWA.

7. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. The current version is always published on this page.

8. Contact

Questions, requests or reports: supportpnx007@gmail.com
